TL;DR: MotionRep jointly generates future DINO representations and 3D object flow within a unified diffusion transformer, modeling future states in a semantically and geometrically rich representation space rather than a pixel-reconstruction-oriented latent space to achieve accurate 3D object flow generation and strong generalization to unseen scenes with only three denoising steps.

Abstract

Many embodied world models predict future states in latent spaces optimized for RGB reconstruction, which do not explicitly prioritize object-level semantics and geometry relevant to motion generation. We present MotionRep, a visual representation world model that jointly generates future DINO representations and 3D object flow, coupling semantic and geometric information with object-centric dynamics. To make high-dimensional DINO features amenable to generative modeling, we introduce DINO-VAE, which combines temporal and channel compression with latent regularization to produce compact latents while preserving semantic and geometric information. Conditioned on an initial observation, object query points, and a language instruction, a unified diffusion transformer predicts future visual representations and 3D object flow through joint attention. MotionRep employs asymmetric modality-specific noise schedules to keep the visual stream ahead of 3D object flow during joint denoising, allowing progressively refined future visual representations to guide flow generation. Experiments on simulated and real-world manipulation benchmarks demonstrate that MotionRep outperforms pixel-based baselines in 3D object flow accuracy and generalization to unseen scenes, while requiring only three denoising steps for inference. These results support predictive visual representations as an effective future-state space for object-centric dynamics modeling.

Quantitative Results

We evaluate 3D object flow generation on WorldFlowBench, comprising 1,678 manipulation clips across three groups: Sim7 (396), Real3 (882), and SpatialCog (400). Given an initial RGB observation, a language instruction, and 64 object query points, each method predicts 3D object trajectories over a 49-frame sequence. MotionRep consistently achieves the best ADE, FDE, and PWT across all three groups, outperforming advanced baselines.

Representation Analysis of DINO-VAE

DINO-VAE produces compact generative latents while preserving rich semantic and geometric information. We evaluate its representation quality through semantic probing, geometric probing, and reconstruction-based downstream tasks.

Semantic structure of the latent space. Following the DINOv3 protocol, we train a linear probe on UCF101 for action classification. DINO-VAE latents achieve substantially higher classification accuracy than pixel-reconstruction-oriented video VAE latents, demonstrating their ability to preserve high-level semantics under strong compression.

Bidirectional Dynamics Inference

Beyond joint generation, MotionRep supports bidirectional conditional dynamics inference within a unified model. Under visual-conditioned inverse dynamics, MotionRep generates 3D object flow conditioned on clean future DINO representations, outperforming Track4World on Sim7. Under flow-conditioned forward dynamics, MotionRep generates future DINO representations conditioned on clean 3D object flow, modeling scene evolution under specified object motion.
